# Velora — LinkedIn About

I built Velora from the ground up — a premium fashion e-commerce platform that ships with full English/Spanish bilingual support and payment methods native to the Colombian market (PSE, Nequi, Bre-B, credit cards via Wompi). It's a monorepo spanning a Next.js 15 storefront, a Fastify API, and a React Native mobile app, with Supabase auth, Cloudinary media, real-time inventory management, and an admin dashboard. Every detail — from the gold-on-ivory palette to the "Wear your identity" brand voice — was intentional.

**Skills to tag:** Next.js, React, Fastify, PostgreSQL, React Native, TypeScript, Turborepo, E-commerce, Supabase, Product Design
